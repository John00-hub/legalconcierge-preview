MCIArb · A.C.I.S · Founder and CEO

Corporate Social Responsibility

The Young Wigs Conference.

From 13 people in a room, to a community of 12,000.

The Young Wigs Conference is the first and largest gathering of young lawyers, entrepreneurs and innovation enthusiasts — analysing the convergence of law, business and technology, and helping lawyers articulate a pathway into technology as a business. Nine editions have held across Kano, Abuja, Port Harcourt and Lagos, with tuition scholarships awarded to law students along the way.
